BotaniGard (GHA): 4.4×10¹⁰ conidia/g or 2.16×10¹⁰ conidia/mL; 4.0–8.0×10¹² conidia/ha; Laverlam; 2016 registrations included Spain, Greece, Italy, Germany, Austria among others (Mascarin2016 via the EU-strain note).

Naturalis-L (ATCC 74040): 2.3–6.9×10⁷ conidia/mL; 2.3–6.9×10¹⁰ conidia/ha; Troy Biosciences; 2016 table included Austria, Italy, Spain, Greece (Mascarin2016 via the EU-strain note). Sybilska reports Naturalis (ATCC 74040) reducing tomato russet mite (*Aculops lycopersici*) under direct application, with residual activity as a stated limit.

Ostrinil (147): 5.8×10⁸ conidia/g; 25 kg/ha corn or 4–10 g/palm; Arysta; France in the 2016 table (EU-strain note).

Entostat Bb38 (IMI389521): wax-based electrostatic micropowder for grain-storage arthropods (Mascarin2016). 2016 listing was UK; EU active-substance approval followed in 2019.

PPRI 5339: BroadBand EC, min. 4×10⁹ viable spores/mL, South Africa in 2016; EU Member State product authorisations **[to be verified]** (EU-strain note). Velifer ES is the named EU-market example in README.

Nouri-Aiin2021: recultured BotaniGard >70% earthworm mortality in 4 weeks; label-as-prescribed <60%; 1-year refrigerated spores <20% and similar to controls.

Weeks2016: BotaniGard ES, Mycotrol O and Met52 EC raised house-fly mortality on days 4–9 relative to balEnce and control; balEnce failed to germinate in one preliminary trial and <20% in another.

Smith2000: BotaniGard (GHA) on four coccinellid species in lab spray; mortality up to 100% (interiorscape nontarget proxy).

Sybilska2025: Naturalis (ATCC 74040) on tomato russet mite (*Aculops lycopersici*); mortality up to 90% in lab direct application.

Chavez2023: at 2.5 ± 0.1 × 10⁶ spores/mL, Velifer ES and BotaniGard ES killed tea shot-hole borer faster (6–8 d) than WP products (10–11 d); cadaver sporulation highest on Velifer ES.

Parker2015: BotaniGard 22WP and ES at 1 × 10⁷ conidia mL⁻¹, 67–80% mortality at 9 d and 95–100% at 12 d on second-instar *Halyomorpha halys*.

Prince2020: Botanigard ES produced 639 viable spores per mm² and more *Myzus persicae* mortality than the other fungal products in that trial.

Chandler2005: isolate 432.99 cultured from Naturalis-L was pathogenic to *Tetranychus urticae*; glasshouse sprays outperformed laboratory bioassays.

Clavet2013: BotaniGard oil carrier alone gave 96% *Listronotus maculicollis* mortality at 1 d in 1 mL water; GHA infection showed later (77% at 10 d in 0.5 mL water).

Clifton2020: BoteGHA and Aprehend; foliage CFUs 5–7 d; direct Aprehend kill attributed to oil, then infection.

Shikano2021: Aprehend residues vs household chemicals; 12 of 22 chemicals reduced spore viability at 5 weeks; 7-week Aprehend mortality still reported as usable.

Erler2015: BroadBand (PPRI 5339, min. 4 × 10⁹ conidia/mL) up to 79.8% young and 71.6% older *Polyphylla fullo* larval mortality.

Mantzoukas2022: GHA (Botanigard) and PPRI 5339 (Velifer ES) plus wild AP0101 as endophytes on melon and strawberry.

Antwi: BotaniGard slower than Helix XTra, Capture and SpinTor on crucifer flea beetle (LT50 grouping).

Aristizabal2017 (nursery rose spray proxy; abstract; PDF on disk is image-only): BotaniGard ES (GHA) and Met-52 EC (F52) at label rates each cut *Scirtothrips dorsalis* counts 48–71% over 4–6 weeks under simulated nursery conditions; spinosad reached 95% (Aristizabal2017).

Aristizabal2018 (pre-shipping dip proxy; abstract): BotaniGard (*B. bassiana*) dip on mint cut whitefly corrected mortality to 81% alone and 86% with Natur'l Oil; Preferal (*Isaria fumosorosea*) also reduced *Bemisia tabaci* versus control on shipped plants (Aristizabal2018).

Representative EU dossiers cover conventional uses (protected crops, storage, palm). Trap, bait-station and autodissemination as labelled uses: **[to be verified]**.
