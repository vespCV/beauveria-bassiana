## Catch-treat-release and bait (chemical templates)

Buczkowski2024: *Vespula maculifrons* donors sprayed with 130 μL of 0.5% fipronil; laboratory secondary mortality; field catch-treat-release transferred insecticide. Agent: fipronil.

Barandika2023: protein bait 0.01% fipronil; 90 beekeeper questionnaires; groups with 10–30 and >30 hornets showed a drop lasting at least two weeks; ~75% reduction in the >30 group; pupae hatching after baiting explained later rebounds. Co-authors RF and EA employed by the bait maker.

Edwards2017: Vespex 0.1% fipronil; 93% traffic drop in four days at one site; >97% after 20–38 days at others.

Poidatz2018 transfer route mortality index 0.26 vs 0.60 direct (chapter 02). Authors discuss a Trojan-horse path: trap-and-contaminate workers, or protein bait for larvae (adults take carbohydrate for self).

Brownbridge2009: protein bait with wasp-active *Metarhizium* and *B. bassiana* isolates stayed attractive to foraging *Vespula*; nest traffic fell significantly under *Metarhizium*; infected larvae recovered from nests exposed to each fungal treatment (NZ poster abstract).
