## *Vespa velutina*

Poidatz2019 describes a Bb strain from a naturally infected foundress in Bretagne: morphology, molecular ID, mycelium growth versus temperature (fastest 20-28 °C; optimum about 20-22.6 °C). Likely uptake during hibernal diapause in soil or wood, or during wood-pulp collection. Application above 20 °C recommended. First-author funding: Bayer Crop Science (Poidatz2019). Bioassay mortality, LT50 and LC50 sit in Poidatz2018 (PDF on disk). The 2019 paper is the strain description.

Poidatz2018 bioassays of indigenous French isolates (including isolate B) on adult *V. velutina* at about 10⁷ spores/mL, 23 °C, groups of five. Four routes: direct immersion, walking on a treated surface, food, and inter-individual transfer.

| Route | Mortality index | n | SD | Cite |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Food | 0.14 | 38 | 0.04 | Poidatz2018 Table 1 |
| Transfer | 0.26 | 38 | 0.04 | Poidatz2018 Table 1 |
| Contact | 0.35 | 38 | 0.04 | Poidatz2018 Table 1 |
| Direct | 0.60 | 41 | 0.04 | Poidatz2018 Table 1 |

LT50 across isolates 5.8 ± 0.44 d; isolate B 6.25 ± 0.67 d (Poidatz2018). LC50: **[to be verified]** (absent from the extracted PDF text).

Lacombrade2025 compared *V. velutina*, *Vespula vulgaris* and *Bombus terrestris* after *Metarhizium robertsii* exposure: hornets had lower survival even at low spore concentrations. Funding ADEME/ANRT CIFRE; M2i Biocontrol stated as non-influential on analysis (Lacombrade2025). Agent: *M. robertsii*.

Santos2026: bee and bumblebee pathogens in 38 *V. velutina* nests across larvae, pupae, adults and queens (shared nest compartment). Turchi2018 reviews control options; poisoned baits scored high efficiency for *Vespula*, unknown for *V. velutina*. Barandika2023, Edwards2017, Buczkowski2024 are chemical-bait or catch-treat-release designs (chapter 04).
