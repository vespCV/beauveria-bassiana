# 5 Fungal growth on the Vvel

Research question 5: are nest temperature and humidity sufficient for germination, cuticle breach and sporulation after death.

## Laboratory optima for Bb

| Parameter | Value | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Optimum T | about 23–28 °C | Zimmermann2007 |
| Minimum T | about 5–10 °C | Zimmermann2007 |
| Maximum T | about 30–38 °C, isolate-dependent | Zimmermann2007 |
| Moisture | germination on cuticle and cadaver sporulation need high moisture | Zimmermann2007 |
| Macrohumidity | infections recorded at 60–70% | Zimmermann2007 |
| UV half-life | about 2 h after simulated sunlight (Ignoffo and Garcia 1992) | Zimmermann2007 |
| Soil storage 4 °C | Bb isolate still viable after six years in sterile soil | Zimmermann2007 |
| Soil 15 °C | little loss of viability | Zimmermann2007 |
| Soil 55 °C | conidia unrecoverable after 10 days | Zimmermann2007 |

Poidatz2019 foundress isolate: fastest mycelial growth 20–28 °C; optimum about 20–22.6 °C; field application above 20 °C recommended for installation and dispersion.

Fu2026 strain WZS5 (from *Zeugodacus cucurbitae*): at 30 °C, median germination 7.7 h, sporulation 1.1 × 10⁸ conidia mL⁻¹, radial growth 4.8 mm d⁻¹; LC50 1.32 × 10⁷; LT50 5.28 d at 1.0 × 10⁸; activity retained at 35 °C; horizontal transmission effective at 30 °C.

Kim2010: corn-oil coating raised granular thermotolerance (dry-heat LT50 8.68 h at 50 °C).

Costa2001: UV-blocking greenhouse film (<380 nm) doubled germination at one week vs <360 nm films.

Mascarin2016: oil formulations for low RH, heat and solar radiation.

Fang2012 (engineered UV tolerance; mosquito proxy): seven hours of natural sunlight (11:00–18:00) killed about 60% of photolyase-knockout *Metarhizium* spores and slowed germination of survivors. Expressing *Halobacterium* CPD photolyase HsPHR2 in *B. bassiana* increased photorepair >30-fold; 100% of transgenic spores survived 4 h sunlight versus 10% wild type, and 31% survived 7 h versus 0.7% wild type. Transgenic *B. bassiana* kept virulence on *Anopheles gambiae* after solar exposure that abolished kill by irradiated wild-type spores (Fang2012).

## Nest climate

*V. velutina* nest temperature and relative humidity: **[to be verified]**.

FeasSanchez2019 describes winter nest architecture and caste counts in Galicia (three nests). Pupae in capped cells were a large fraction in nest A (48% of whole-colony count).

VanZyl2024: *P. dominula* nest paper hydrophobic; pupae little infected after inundative spray (3.42% ± 0.68% in the EPF/EPN mix). After 144 h, Bb-treated larvae and pupae were moved to humid wells to score fungal growth.

Omuse2022: hive-simulated 30 ± 2 °C, 60–80% RH; authors expect field and nest conditions to lower fungal effect via grooming and central-nest temperatures.

Meikle: daily Varroa fall peaked 5–8 d after hive treatment at conditions matching a 30 °C / 80% RH bioassay.

Leite2022: sporulation on bee eyes confirmed after humid-chamber incubation (white-eye mycosis).

Peng2020ao (honey-bee nest near 35 °C stopping germination) is cited in README. No PDF on disk; **[to be verified]** against full text when acquired.

Romo-Chacon2016: EPF growth 25-30 °C; inhibited cooler than 15 °C or warmer than 33 °C; both fungi needed >90% RH.
