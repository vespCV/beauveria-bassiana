Research question 3: how to deliver Bb so *V. velutina* can take conidia with her (formulation, dispenser, bait).

Two pickup modes sit side by side: contact on a passage surface (1- or 2-way dispenser), and inoculum in sugar or protein bait.

## Dispensers (pollinator vectoring)

Hive-mounted 1- and 2-way dispensers load bumblebees or honey bees, which then vector Bb onto crops (Al-Mazraawi2006; Kapongo2025; Kotomale2024). That is delivery-system evidence on hairy pollinators.

| Finding | Detail | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Load on bees | 97% then 99% of collected *B. impatiens* with detectable Bb | Al-Mazraawi2006 |
| Crop / pest | TPB 42% then 30% with detectable Bb; WFT infection 40% and 34% vs 3% | Al-Mazraawi2006 |
| Carrier | Maize flour carried more conidia than wheat, semolina, corn meal, potato starch, oat or barley | Al-Mazraawi2007 |
| Particle size | Finer, drier carrier and higher conidial density raised load | Al-Mazraawi2007 |
| Pass time | Acquisition independent of time in the dispenser | Al-Mazraawi2007 |
| Product | BotaniGard 22WP GHA, 4.4 × 10¹⁰ conidia/kg; 8 g per dispenser fill; 95–99% viability | Kapongo2025 |
| T5 BVT | 1.46 × 10⁹ conidia/g reduced *R. padi* vs untreated | Kapongo2025 |
| Strawberry mite | *B. terrestris* disseminated inoculum onto plants | Kotomale2024 |

## Oils, wax, UV

Oil formulations improve infection at low humidity, heat tolerance and survival under solar radiation (Mascarin2016). Clavet2013: BotaniGard carrier oil caused 96% weevil mortality at 24 h in 1 mL water (physical oil kill). Clifton2020: immediate Aprehend mortality from oil. Muniz2020: carnauba wax in dry powder raised *B. bassiana* virulence against *Lucilia sericata*; wax improved conidial attachment to cuticle (blowfly formulation proxy). Kim2010: corn-oil coating of millet granules, dry-heat LT50 8.68 h at 50 °C, better than sunflower or cottonseed.

Costa2001: greenhouse plastic blocking UV <380 nm kept germination at least twice as high at 1 week as plastics blocking only <360 nm.

Shikano2021: household chemical residues vs Aprehend viability at 5 weeks.

Mascarin2016: Entostat electrostatic wax/kaolin micropowder for grain arthropods; reflective minerals as UV-blockers; shelf life tied to <5% moisture, low temperature, reduced oxygen.

Kirsch2022: UV degradation reduced by trap placement.

Fang2012 (transgenic UV proxy): archaeal CPD photolyase (HsPHR2) in *B. bassiana* raised photorepair >30-fold; after 4 h natural sunlight 100% of Bb-HsPHR2 spores survived versus 10% wild type; after 7 h 31% versus 0.7% (44-fold). Solar-exposed Bb-HsPHR2 retained virulence against *Anopheles gambiae* where wild-type spores lost kill (Fang2012).

Meikle: UV cuts conidial longevity on foraging bees.
