# Consensus.app is a coverage check, not a parallel review

Before locking database runs, Consensus.app is used only to surface the most important papers per screening pillar so categories, research questions, and search strings can be gap-checked against the applied motivation. Copilot/Pro prompts are delivery-first (_B. bassiana_ with traps, baits, autodissemination, and fluid/protein carriers), mapped 1:1 to pillars `01`–`05` with separate Copilots for `02a`/`02b`/`02c`, and skip `06-background-proxies` unless upstream pillars keep producing background-only themes. Each Copilot returns a ranked paper list plus vocabulary (strain codes, product names, delivery terms). Hits are triaged: theme gaps revise questions or categories; missing synonyms/strains/products revise strings; out-of-scope papers are ignored. Pillar `01` uses Consensus for device/formulation/strain literature and a mandatory parallel pass of EPA, EU Pesticide Database, CABI, and patent sources for the market catalogue. Rejected alternatives: treating Consensus as the systematic corpus or PRISMA evidence; Yes/No or Relationship modes with hard efficacy thresholds; social-Hymenoptera-first framing that conflicts with inclusion scope; a Consensus pass for `06` that floods pathogenesis noise.
