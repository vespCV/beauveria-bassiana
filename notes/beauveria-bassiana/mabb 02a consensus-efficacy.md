
# **B. bassiana** Mortality and LT50 via Non-Foliar Delivery

The 20 retrieved studies do not report _Beauveria bassiana_ delivered through traps, bait stations, autodissemination devices, or contaminated foragers. All tested delivery methods are foliar sprays, direct-contact bioassays, endophytic inoculation, or treated-food exposure. The available evidence therefore characterizes mortality and LT50/LT90 under these conventional routes but cannot directly answer the question about alternative delivery platforms.

## Endophytic and Treated-Food Delivery

Endophytic colonization of maize by _B. bassiana_ achieved 56.8% mortality in _S. frugiperda_ larvae fed colonized plants, compared with 77.2% in direct-contact assays [Monica2024](https://consensus.app/papers/native-beauveria-bassiana-isolates-harnessing-monica-rajanikanth/52538700562a51f4b112b55dd385248d/). In cabbage, endophytic inoculation via seed coating and root soaking produced corrected mortalities ≤57.6% against _P. xylostella_ larvae, significantly lower than foliar spraying (≥96.7%) [Heviefo2020](https://consensus.app/papers/comparative-efficacy-of-endophytic-versus-foliar-heviefo-nyamador/a3e529d197bc5a3189da930a88ec1ccd/). Treated-food delivery to _C. italicus_ nymphs yielded dose-dependent mortality with LT50 values of 3.1–4.5 days depending on instar [Mantzoukas2026](https://consensus.app/papers/effect-of-the-entomopathogenic-fungus-beauveria-bassiana-mantzoukas-stamou/9945a470fd8558518fc90796641a2a18/).

## Sublethal and Colony-Level Effects

- **Reduced fecundity** in _S. exigua_: LC50-treated females laid 224 eggs vs. 360 in controls [Zafar2024](https://consensus.app/papers/twosex-life-table-analysis-for-optimizing-beauveria-zafar-shoukat/7a9401fcadca5f4ab160372c7f05ddbb/).
- **Pupal and adult deformities** plus decreased adult emergence in _S. litura_ treated with isolate TGS2.3 [Islam2023](https://consensus.app/papers/biocontrol-potential-of-native-isolates-of-beauveria-islam-chowdhury/eaa31713945053f79bb0391fb8b57b0c/).
- **Transgenerational effects**: F1 offspring of _B. bassiana_-exposed _S. exigua_ showed reduced intrinsic rate of increase and net reproduction rate [Zafar2024](https://consensus.app/papers/twosex-life-table-analysis-for-optimizing-beauveria-zafar-shoukat/7a9401fcadca5f4ab160372c7f05ddbb/).
- **Reduced feeding efficacy** (69–78%) in _S. frugiperda_ second-instar larvae within 48 h of treatment [Idrees2022](https://consensus.app/papers/bioassays-of-beauveria-bassiana-isolates-against-the-fall-idrees-afzal/4ab12ac12b69545791da5a9d97e1c74e/).

The retrieved literature provides no data on trap-based, bait-station, autodissemination, or contaminated-forager delivery of _B. bassiana_. All mortality, LT50/LT90, and colony-level effects reported here derive from spray, contact, endophytic, or treated-food exposure — the alternative delivery modalities specified in the question remain untested in this corpus.
