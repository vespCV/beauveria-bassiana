**Detection of bee‐associated pathogens in different developmental stages within the nest of the invasive hornet Vespa velutina**

- DWV and other pathogens were detected across all developmental stages, indicating widespread pathogen detection and suggesting multiple exposure routes within the nest with **possible vertical transmission**.
