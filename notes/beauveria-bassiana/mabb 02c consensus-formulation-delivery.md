# Carrier Effects on **B. bassiana** Spore Performance

Oil-based carriers, microencapsulation, and wettable powder formulations each enhance different aspects of _Beauveria bassiana_ conidial viability, germination, persistence, and field delivery, with trade-offs depending on the target pest and application context [Kolanchi2025](https://consensus.app/papers/bioprocess-and-genetic-advances-enhancing-beauveria-kolanchi-saminathan/0d6733aa5f765583982ceb952deadedb/)[Lopes2019](https://consensus.app/papers/influence-of-two-formulation-types-and-moisture-levels-on-lopes-faria/561143dc41a052b4923d185674dd5509/).

## Oil-Based Carriers: Viability and Germination

Vegetable and mineral oils consistently outperform aqueous carriers for maintaining conidial viability during storage. Virgin coconut oil preserved 69.2% germination after 35 days versus 25% in water controls [Azurah2026](https://consensus.app/papers/carrier-formulations-to-enhance-conidial-viability-and-azurah-daud/e0fe866f96cf5b6190e77a195313b800/). Paraffin oil similarly maintained 55.4% germination under the same conditions [Azurah2026](https://consensus.app/papers/carrier-formulations-to-enhance-conidial-viability-and-azurah-daud/e0fe866f96cf5b6190e77a195313b800/). A hydrogenated rapeseed oil fat pellet retained 84.7% germination after 45 days at 25°C, compared with 55.3% for an oil suspension formulation [Hidalgo1998](https://consensus.app/papers/the-effect-of-different-formulations-of-beauveria-hidalgo-moore/85309af3aef85dbb88651349bf69e691/). Emulsion formulations with linseed oil achieved 94% conidial germination when freshly prepared [Mishra2013](https://consensus.app/papers/preparation-characterization-and-insecticidal-activity-mishra-kumar/eb169185f2925e12a698514ee9b91d22/).

Residual water in oil formulations degrades shelf life: drying soybean oil before formulation reduced conidial mortality during storage [Lopes2019](https://consensus.app/papers/influence-of-two-formulation-types-and-moisture-levels-on-lopes-faria/561143dc41a052b4923d185674dd5509/). Synthetic oil (ShellSol T) improved spore persistence on water surfaces and reduced _Anopheles gambiae_ pupation by 39–50% under field conditions [Bukhari2011](https://consensus.app/papers/development-of-metarhizium-anisopliae-and-beauveria-bukhari-takken/e9241eb605ba5dc89ec1147ed4866447/).

## Microencapsulation: Thermal and UV Protection

Microencapsulation in biopolymer matrices protects conidia from heat, UV radiation, and desiccation while enabling sustained release. Sodium alginate/maltodextrin microparticles retained 89.5% conidial viability and provided **thermal protection at 45°C** [De Jesus Seabra2024](https://consensus.app/papers/new-beauveria-bassiana-aerial-conidiabased-seabra-santos/32334f35bf2654b298803e7828c5af46/). Spray-dried microparticles of dextrin, skimmed milk, and PVP K90 maintained 80% viability after six months at 4°C [Liu2009](https://consensus.app/papers/lowtemperature-spray-drying-for-the-microencapsulation-liu-liu/fa9c85cb9b235da7823e1fd7ea71adaa/). Both spray-drying and ionic gelation produced conidia more stable to temperature and UV than unformulated controls [Felizatti2021](https://consensus.app/papers/encapsulation-of-b-bassiana-in-biopolymers-improving-felizatti-manzano/003fd13f1c905df49a83403551c98619/).

Encapsulated formulations enhanced pathogenicity: microparticle-encapsulated _B. bassiana_ achieved 83.1% mortality against _Plutella xylostella_ versus 64.8% for non-encapsulated conidia [De Jesus Seabra2024](https://consensus.app/papers/new-beauveria-bassiana-aerial-conidiabased-seabra-santos/32334f35bf2654b298803e7828c5af46/). Even low conidial concentrations in microencapsulated formulations yielded high mite mortality [De Oliveira2023](https://consensus.app/papers/encapsulation-of-beauveria-bassiana-in-oliveira-oliveira/d03742695045574fae835c1a5a236cfb/). Spray-dried formulations improved aqueous dispersion, reducing the need for field-application additives [Felizatti2021](https://consensus.app/papers/encapsulation-of-b-bassiana-in-biopolymers-improving-felizatti-manzano/003fd13f1c905df49a83403551c98619/).

## Wettable Powder and Surfactant Formulations

Wettable powder formulations provided significant field control of _P. xylostella_ on crucifers, with persistence on leaves ratedependent and declining over 3–4 days post-spray [Vandenberg1998](https://consensus.app/papers/assessment-of-beauveria-bassiana-sprays-for-control-of-vandenberg-shelton/3756381c9831544bb10666d9e6d5e4f6/). Two applications of wettable powder at higher rates outperformed single applications [Vandenberg1998](https://consensus.app/papers/assessment-of-beauveria-bassiana-sprays-for-control-of-vandenberg-shelton/3756381c9831544bb10666d9e6d5e4f6/). Conidial hydrophobicity necessitates surfactant addition for aqueous dispersion, but surfactant choice critically affects viability [Melo2020](https://consensus.app/papers/selection-of-surfactants-for-enhancing-the-wettability-of-melo-zotarelli/71c18322be7d5d81844a30a6d5a481d5/).

- **Surfom D** preserved 55% germination after three months versus 0% in surfactant-free controls [Melo2020](https://consensus.app/papers/selection-of-surfactants-for-enhancing-the-wettability-of-melo-zotarelli/71c18322be7d5d81844a30a6d5a481d5/).
- **Emulsogen TS 200** reduced surface tension to 38 mN/m but decreased fungal viability [Melo2020](https://consensus.app/papers/selection-of-surfactants-for-enhancing-the-wettability-of-melo-zotarelli/71c18322be7d5d81844a30a6d5a481d5/).
- Non-ionic surfactants (Tween 80, Tween 20, Ballista, Break-thru) maintained or enhanced conidial viability at 0.025% [Zeina2025](https://consensus.app/papers/evaluation-of-fifteen-adjuvants-for-compatibility-with-zeina-laing/aabb95bbd8ac5f2baeead6a3a69f1124/).
- Break-thru, Tween 20, and Ballista enhanced **conidial adhesion and penetration** in tick larvae [Zeina2025](https://consensus.app/papers/evaluation-of-fifteen-adjuvants-for-compatibility-with-zeina-laing/aabb95bbd8ac5f2baeead6a3a69f1124/).

Water-dispersible granule formulations showed 10–15% lower insecticidal activity than oil dispersion or unformulated conidia against the coffee berry borer [Lopes2019](https://consensus.app/papers/influence-of-two-formulation-types-and-moisture-levels-on-lopes-faria/561143dc41a052b4923d185674dd5509/).

## Traps, Bait Stations, and Autodissemination Devices

Formulated _B. bassiana_ delivered through traps and autodissemination devices achieves effective pest transfer and field persistence. An oil-based attract-and-infect device for _Rhynchophorus ferrugineus_ maintained 50% conidial viability for up to two months and still caused 50% mortality after exposure to 2.5-month field-aged devices [Dembilio2018](https://consensus.app/papers/development-of-an-attractandinfect-system-to-control-dembilio-moya/e04440fc3acb51989da42eabeb7b3692/). Microgranular formulations with sugar attracted _Aedes aegypti_ adults and achieved 42.2% mortality in traps, supporting autodissemination potential among surviving adults [Zamora-Avilés2022](https://consensus.app/papers/increased-attraction-and-stability-of-beauveria-zamora-avil%C3%A9s-orozco-flores/8ee74d40edef5868a89450a04e6baa60/). Conidial stability in microgranules was higher than in additive-only treatments regardless of storage temperature [Zamora-Avilés2022](https://consensus.app/papers/increased-attraction-and-stability-of-beauveria-zamora-avil%C3%A9s-orozco-flores/8ee74d40edef5868a89450a04e6baa60/).

Hydrogel-based delivery systems using hydroxyethylcellulose sustained _B. bassiana_ growth for up to 24 days and reduced _Aedes albopictus_ egg hatching by over 90% through combined mechanical and fungal action [Friuli2024](https://consensus.app/papers/beauveria-bassiana-associated-with-a-novel-biomimetic-friuli-lia/1d7ed52a270a5900bae8202294da057f/). Tea waste-based bait formulations achieved 100% housefly mortality in traps within 60 hours, though storage stability was limited [Mishra2013](https://consensus.app/papers/preparation-characterization-and-insecticidal-activity-mishra-kumar/eb169185f2925e12a698514ee9b91d22/). Emulsifiable concentrate formulations photostabilized conidia, maintaining 68.9% germination after nine minutes of UV-B irradiation while unformulated conidia completely lost germinability [García Riaño2021](https://consensus.app/papers/actividad-insecticida-y-persistencia-de-un-formulado-a-ria%C3%B1o-g%C3%B3mez-valderrama/a4d4334a14bb5eaa9481daaaa035a4be/).

## Cross-Cutting Patterns and Caveats

No single carrier optimizes all performance metrics simultaneously. Oil-based carriers excel in storage viability and aquatic persistence but can leave oily residues on grain and may not improve efficacy when mineral oil alone is insecticidal [Hidalgo1998](https://consensus.app/papers/the-effect-of-different-formulations-of-beauveria-hidalgo-moore/85309af3aef85dbb88651349bf69e691/). Microencapsulation provides superior thermal and UV protection but introduces processing complexity and can reduce wettability [De Jesus Seabra2024](https://consensus.app/papers/new-beauveria-bassiana-aerial-conidiabased-seabra-santos/32334f35bf2654b298803e7828c5af46/). Wettable powders offer practical field application but show shorter foliar persistence and slightly lower insecticidal activity than oil-based alternatives [Lopes2019](https://consensus.app/papers/influence-of-two-formulation-types-and-moisture-levels-on-lopes-faria/561143dc41a052b4923d185674dd5509/)[Vandenberg1998](https://consensus.app/papers/assessment-of-beauveria-bassiana-sprays-for-control-of-vandenberg-shelton/3756381c9831544bb10666d9e6d5e4f6/). Surfactant compatibility is formulation-specific: the same surfactant can enhance adhesion while reducing viability, necessitating empirical screening for each strain and application context [Zeina2025](https://consensus.app/papers/evaluation-of-fifteen-adjuvants-for-compatibility-with-zeina-laing/aabb95bbd8ac5f2baeead6a3a69f1124/).
