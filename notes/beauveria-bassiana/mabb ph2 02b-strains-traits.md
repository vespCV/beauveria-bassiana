**Evaluation of Spore Acquisition, Spore Production, and Host Survival Time for Tea Shot-Hole Borer, Euwallacea perbrevis, Adults after Exposure to Four Commercial Products Containing Beauveria bassiana**
- BioCeres WP, BotaniGard WP, BotaniGard ES and Velifer ES.
- Beetles exposed to Velifer ES and BotaniGard ES died faster (6–8 days)
- Spore production on cadavers of beetles dipped in Velifer ES was the highest among all treatments, whereas it was lowest on cadavers of beetles dipped in BotaniGard ES
