**Possible pathogens of social wasps (vespidae)**
- 1999 and not much has changed
- potential pathogens
	- fungus
	- Aspergillus flavus and Beauveria bassiana have been confirmed pathogenic
	- Metarhizium anisopliae pathogenic in Vespula sp
	- Other fungi act as saprophytes
	- Over 80 pathogens in 
	- Challenges
		- delivery system
	- Nest failure due to disease has seldom been recorded
		- fungi penetrate the cuticle directly
		- can spread by surface contact
		- aerial spore dispersal between layers of comb in horizontally combs
	- Bacteria, viruses and protozoa 
		- diseased individuals are likely to be removed from a nest before they can infect others
	- If disease does enter and spread
		- the mortality of a large number of colony members --> worker reproduction is increased to compensate
		- capped cells may provide refugia from pathogens for developing pupae
	- Saliva
		- conjungated noradrenalin = antimicrobial
		- ant (and wasp?) venom = antimicrobial
	- Worker removal, insulation of dead and diseased may restrict spread
