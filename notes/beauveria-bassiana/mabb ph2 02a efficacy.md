**Using Autumnal Trap Crops to Manage Tarnished Plant Bugs (Lygus lineolaris)**
- A common strategy is to minimize movement and hibernate near autumn food sources.
- autumn trap crop for Lygus lineolaris
- we tested four treatments applied to the autumn trap crops to reduce L. lineolaris winter survivorship: (1) hot water, (2) a pathogen (Beauveria bassiana), (3) insecticide (cypermethrin), and (4) a control.
