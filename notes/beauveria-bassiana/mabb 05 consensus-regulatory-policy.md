# **Beauveria bassiana** EU Registration, Data Gaps, and IPM Delivery Systems

In the EU, _Beauveria bassiana_ is regulated as a microbial pesticide active substance under Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009, with the Netherlands serving as rapporteur Member State for at least three strain approvals [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/)[Arena2017](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-arena-auteri/d7431fecfad05200959ee06be84b36ab/)[Arena2018](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-arena-auteri/14ae40e053425dc4ab78b96dba4aba10/). Three strains — 203, IMI389521, and PPRI 5339 — have undergone EFSA peer review, each with distinct representative uses and identified data gaps [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/)[Arena2017](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-arena-auteri/d7431fecfad05200959ee06be84b36ab/)[Arena2018](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-arena-auteri/14ae40e053425dc4ab78b96dba4aba10/).

## Data Requirements and Identified Gaps

EFSA identified critical data gaps for strain 203, including insufficient information on toxicity, infectivity, and pathogenicity to aquatic organisms, as well as toxicity to fish and algae from the secondary metabolite beauvericin [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/). These gaps applied to all representative permanent greenhouse uses [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/).

- **Sensitisation risk**: _B. bassiana_, irrespective of strain, is considered a **potential human allergen** by both skin and inhalation exposure [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/).
- **Acute toxicity**: Oral LD50 exceeded 1.98 × 10⁸ CFU/rat and intratracheal LC50 exceeded 1.8 × 10⁸ CFU/rat, with complete clearance and no evidence of toxicity, pathogenicity, or infectivity [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/).
- **No toxicological thresholds required**: AOEL, ADI, and ARfD were not established and not required for strain 203 [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/).
- **Efficacy confirmed**: The product containing strain 203 was deemed **sufficiently effective** with no phytotoxicity or undesirable side-effects observed [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/).

Registration of microbial control agents requires ensuring safety for non-target organisms, especially predatory insects and parasitoids [Fabrice2020](https://consensus.app/papers/toward-the-efficient-use-of-beauveria-bassiana-in-fabrice-elie/518917c3ec0a5a6294fd7694419a10e4/). Worldwide registration guidelines for entomopathogenic fungi focus on similar safety issues encompassing identity, biological properties, environmental fate, and effects on non-target and vertebrate organisms [Zimmermann2007](https://consensus.app/papers/review-on-safety-of-the-entomopathogenic-fungi-beauveria-zimmermann/381a6df6113e55dbbf5b66911ccde7de/).

## Autodissemination and Trap-Based Delivery Systems

Autoinoculation traps impregnated with _B. bassiana_ conidia have demonstrated high field mortality against key pests. A trap for the coffee berry borer (_Hypothenemus hampei_) achieved 66–92% confirmed field mortality using fabric strips loaded with ~5.44 × 10⁸ conidia/cm² [Mota2017](https://consensus.app/papers/autoinoculation-trap-for-management-of-hypothenemus-mota-silva/63d0caebaf1b5a14b3a731a61706872f/). The FraxiProtec autodissemination device for emerald ash borer infected ~40% of adults at treated sites and reduced population growth by 40%, with infected beetles detected up to 125 m from treatment zones [Srei2020](https://consensus.app/papers/microbial-control-of-the-emerald-ash-borer-coleoptera-srei-guertin/61afc4fcc5105f7e8f680dd08357f34e/).

For Mediterranean fruit fly (_Ceratitis capitata_), both panel-type and cylinder-type autoinoculation devices, as well as sterile male vectors, achieved over 90% total population reduction [Toledo2017](https://consensus.app/papers/pathogenicity-of-three-formulations-of-beauveria-toledo-flores/d0d14583a46b5ad7864592d1c0ca8751/). Sterile males used as conidia vectors represent a novel integration with the sterile insect technique for area-wide management [Toledo2017](https://consensus.app/papers/pathogenicity-of-three-formulations-of-beauveria-toledo-flores/d0d14583a46b5ad7864592d1c0ca8751/).

## IPM Integration and Compatibility

_B. bassiana_ functions best as one tool among many in integrated pest management rather than as a stand-alone approach [Mascarin2016](https://consensus.app/papers/the-production-and-uses-of-beauveria-bassiana-as-a-mascarin-jaronski/df48205aac1e5111ba8906e34634882f/). Its broad host range and eco-friendly profile allow integration with chemical and biological controls to reduce synthetic pesticide reliance [Santhosh2025](https://consensus.app/papers/entomopathogenic-fungi-in-integrated-pest-management-the-santhosh-namachivayam/f436d69ef745501eb87ec8c6279e1353/)[Kumar2024](https://consensus.app/papers/exploring-the-efficacy-of-beauveria-bassiana-a-kumar-saxena/c173b60a022457f0b92e6ffcb6b97293/).

- **Parasitoid compatibility**: _B. bassiana_ exposure did not affect parasitism or survival of the egg parasitoid _Telenomus podisi_, though it influenced sex ratio and emergence [Da Silva2026](https://consensus.app/papers/potential-of-beauveria-bassiana-in-the-control-of-silva-carneiro/0d1c3841380d5c19ba2a745e934c00f6/).
- **Synergistic insecticide combinations**: Emamectin benzoate showed the least inhibition of _B. bassiana_ spore germination and mycelial growth, with synergistic interactions at ratios exceeding 4:1 [Wang2025](https://consensus.app/papers/synergistic-effects-of-beauveria-bassiana-and-wang-li/c1aff5cdf5ea5145b297a3ba5b6480b8/).
- **Formulation advances**: Oil-based emulsions and nanoencapsulation technologies have increased shelf-life and field efficacy, with microencapsulation improving UV protection and prolonging biocontrol duration [Kumar2024](https://consensus.app/papers/exploring-the-efficacy-of-beauveria-bassiana-a-kumar-saxena/c173b60a022457f0b92e6ffcb6b97293/).

Both _B. bassiana_ and _B. brongniartii_ are considered safe based on comprehensive safety data covering effects on non-target microorganisms, plants, soil organisms, aquatic organisms, predators, parasitoids, honey bees, and vertebrates [Zimmermann2007](https://consensus.app/papers/review-on-safety-of-the-entomopathogenic-fungi-beauveria-zimmermann/381a6df6113e55dbbf5b66911ccde7de/). Commercial formulation remains a bottleneck for stable, effective mycoinsecticides in many countries, though good commercial formulations do exist [Mascarin2016](https://consensus.app/papers/the-production-and-uses-of-beauveria-bassiana-as-a-mascarin-jaronski/df48205aac1e5111ba8906e34634882f/).

None of the EFSA peer reviews addressed trap-based, bait-station, or autodissemination delivery as a representative use; all approved or reviewed uses involve conventional inundative spray applications [Anastassiadou2020](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-anastassiadou-arena/82edc646d3e15c74857c1f75edf2dd9c/)[Arena2017](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-arena-auteri/d7431fecfad05200959ee06be84b36ab/)[Arena2018](https://consensus.app/papers/peer-review-of-the-pesticide-risk-assessment-of-the-active-arena-auteri/14ae40e053425dc4ab78b96dba4aba10/). Autodissemination systems would therefore require separate regulatory dossiers and efficacy data packages distinct from existing strain approvals.
