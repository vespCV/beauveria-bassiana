RICANICIDAL Bbas-TR61 (strain KTU-24, oil-based) caused over 97% mortality against Orosanga japonica nymphs and adults in field trials [Biryol2021](https://consensus.app/papers/development-of-mycoinsecticide-formulations-with-biryol-g%C3%BCney/8026ab763a9153488e1f1250b47d96e8/). The same KTU-24 strain was separately formulated as AFIDISIDAL-OD Bbas-TR61, an oil-based mycoinsecticide achieving 82–84% mortality against green peach aphid nymphs [Biryol2022](https://consensus.app/papers/development-of-beauveria-bassiana-ascomycota-biryol-dem%C4%B1rbag/2ba553cde8a65c89a5af7eabdc5de931/). Pa4-OD (strain Pa-4, sunflower oil–based) produced 89.9% field mortality against Thaumetopoea wilkinsoni with minimal effects on beneficial predators [Biryol2025](https://consensus.app/papers/target-and-nontarget-impact-of-oilbased-beauveria-biryol-say%C4%B1n/30f8654e6d0751b9bfc85e4a2aec603a/). BKN20 5%AS (aqueous suspension) outperformed thiamethoxam against the tea mosquito bug, reducing shoot damage by up to 72% [Deka2021](https://consensus.app/papers/beauveria-bassiana-as-a-potential-microbial-biocontrol-deka-babu/ddc210515a8c58f389d7f74b99645649/). Emulsifiable oil formulations of strains Bb and TBb achieved 84–85% control of cereal aphids, comparable to imidacloprid [Qi2024](https://consensus.app/papers/emulsifiable-oilformulated-beauveria-bassiana-competes-qi-shao/1499cc2807795d969a27bd49b0617fce/).

## Formulation Types and Delivery Platforms

B. bassiana conidia are the principal active ingredient, mass-produced primarily via solid-state fermentation yielding hydrophobic aerial conidia, though submerged liquid fermentation offers an alternative for stable dry preparations [Mascarin2016](https://consensus.app/papers/the-production-and-uses-of-beauveria-bassiana-as-a-mascarin-jaronski/df48205aac1e5111ba8906e34634882f/). The most common commercial formulation types identified across 171 mycoinsecticide products are technical concentrates (fungus-colonized substrates, 26.3%), wettable powders (20.5%), and oil dispersions (15.2%) [Faria2007](https://consensus.app/papers/mycoinsecticides-and-mycoacaricides-a-comprehensive-faria-wraight/41ce7e11d36c5989beab004e8fd85255/).

- **Granular formulations** based on ground cornmeal or chicken feed pellets produced 60–98% larval mortality against lesser mealworm in poultry houses [Geden2003](https://consensus.app/papers/evaluation-of-three-formulations-of-beauveria-bassiana-geden-steinkraus/d240b9a1b3395a91a275b4e760d88084/)[Rice2019](https://consensus.app/papers/development-of-mycoinsecticide-formulations-with-rice-baker/34773f5db3a95bf984ffefac7283e559/).
- **Encapsulation** using skimmed milk powder, polyvinyl pyrrolidone, and glucose retained 78% conidial viability after 12 months at 30 °C [Mishra2013](https://consensus.app/papers/preparation-characterization-and-insecticidal-activity-mishra-kumar/eb169185f2925e12a698514ee9b91d22/).
- **Biopolymer carriers** chitooligosaccharide and 2-hydroxyethyl cellulose preserved fungal virulence, achieving 76–85% efficacy against cabbage whitefly [Spasova2026](https://consensus.app/papers/biopolymerbased-formulations-of-beauveria-bassiana-for-spasova-chervenkova/94a1639ae0fb530b962dde04afe92d70/).
- **Oil-based formulations** using canola oil, Codacide™, and linseed oil improved conidial germination and delivery under field conditions [Mishra2013](https://consensus.app/papers/preparation-characterization-and-insecticidal-activity-mishra-kumar/eb169185f2925e12a698514ee9b91d22/)[Rice2019](https://consensus.app/papers/development-of-mycoinsecticide-formulations-with-rice-baker/34773f5db3a95bf984ffefac7283e559/).

Modern formulation approaches also include nanoemulsions and seed-coating technologies designed to improve spore stability and field persistence [Kolanchi2025](https://consensus.app/papers/bioprocess-and-genetic-advances-enhancing-beauveria-kolanchi-saminathan/0d6733aa5f765583982ceb952deadedb/). Formulation remains a bottleneck for commercial B. bassiana mycoinsecticides, with robust downstream processing needed for wider industry adoption [Mascarin2016](https://consensus.app/papers/the-production-and-uses-of-beauveria-bassiana-as-a-mascarin-jaronski/df48205aac1e5111ba8906e34634882f/).
