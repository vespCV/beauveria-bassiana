
## Research question

What evidence exists for the efficacy, strain traits, formulation/delivery, horizontal transmission, non-target risk, and regulatory readiness of _Beauveria bassiana_ when applied via selective traps, bait stations, or autodissemination devices that deliver spores in fluid or protein carriers, such that primarily the target insect acquires and vectors the pathogen?

## Subquestions

**00-existing-products** What commercial _B. bassiana_ products, formulations, or trap/bait/autodissemination systems are already registered or marketed, and which use liquid, oil, protein, or bait-based delivery?

**01-efficacy-mechanics-delivery**

- **01a_efficacy** Can _B. bassiana_ strains delivered via traps, baits, or autodissemination devices produce mortality or population reduction fast enough for quarantine or statutory control timelines?
- **01b_strains_traits** Which strain traits (Pr1 proteases, chitinases, hydrophobins, thermotolerance, UV tolerance, adhesion) enable effective cuticle breach and survival after transfer from fluid/protein carriers or trap surfaces?
- **01c_formulation_delivery** How do fluid, proteinaceous, oil, or microencapsulated carriers affect spore viability, adhesion, germination, and transfer efficiency from traps, baits, or contaminated surfaces under field conditions?

**02-autodissemination-social** How do social behaviours (allogrooming, necrophoresis, trophallaxis) and nest hygiene affect horizontal spore transfer from foragers that contacted a treated trap or bait to nestmates and the queen?

**03-nontarget-ecotox**

- Which physical, chemical, or seasonal attractants and trap/bait designs maximise contact by the target species while minimising exposure of non-target pollinators (_Apis_, _Bombus_, native bees) and beneficial arthropods?
- What is the environmental persistence, secondary transmission risk, and toxicity of _B. bassiana_ strains to non-target arthropods when spores are concentrated in trap or bait matrices?

**04-regulatory-policy** What registration data requirements and pathways exist for _B. bassiana_ products used in traps, bait stations, or autodissemination systems within IPM or emergency/quarantine protocols?

**05-background-proxies** (No primary sub-question — reserve for general biology, pathogenesis, proxy species, and methods papers that are **not** directly about _B. bassiana_ + traps/baits/autodissemination.)
