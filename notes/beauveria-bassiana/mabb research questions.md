
## Research question

What evidence exists for the efficacy, strain traits, formulation/delivery, horizontal transmission, non-target risk, and regulatory readiness of _Beauveria bassiana_ when applied via selective traps, bait stations, or autodissemination devices that deliver spores in fluid or protein carriers, such that primarily the target insect acquires and vectors the pathogen?

## Subquestions
### Market & Existing Products,
- What commercial B. bassiana products, formulations, or trap/bait/autodissemination systems are already registered or marketed, and which use liquid, oil, protein, or bait-based delivery?
### Efficacy vs. Operational Timelines
- Can B. bassiana strains delivered via traps, baits, or autodissemination devices produce mortality or population reduction fast enough for quarantine or statutory control timelines?
### Strain Selection & Cuticular / Physiological Traits
- Which strain traits (Pr1 proteases, chitinases, hydrophobins, thermotolerance, UV tolerance, adhesion) enable effective cuticle breach and survival after transfer from fluid/protein carriers or trap surfaces?
### Formulation, Carrier & Delivery Mechanics
- How do fluid, proteinaceous, oil, or microencapsulated carriers affect spore viability, adhesion, germination, and transfer efficiency from traps, baits, or contaminated surfaces under field conditions?
### Auto-Dissemination & Social Transmission,
- How do social behaviours (allogrooming, necrophoresis, trophallaxis) and nest hygiene affect horizontal spore transfer from foragers that contacted a treated trap or bait to nestmates and the queen?
### Attractants, Trap/Bait Design & Non-Target Selectivity
- Which physical, chemical, or seasonal attractants and trap/bait designs maximise contact by the target species while minimising exposure of non-target pollinators (Apis, Bombus, native bees) and beneficial arthropods?
### Environmental Fate, Secondary Spread & Ecotoxicology
- What is the environmental persistence, secondary transmission risk, and toxicity of B. bassiana strains to non-target arthropods when spores are concentrated in trap or bait matrices?
### Regulatory Pathways & Integration
- What registration data requirements and pathways exist for B. bassiana products used in traps, bait stations, or autodissemination systems within IPM or emergency/quarantine protocols?
