## Applied motivation

End use: _Vespa velutina_ (Asian hornet). Species-specific papers are sparse; they do **not** define inclusion. Screen transferable evidence from other taxa when the intervention or pathway informs selective trap/bait/autodissemination delivery of _B. bassiana_.

Do **not** add a _V. velutina_ / Asian hornet supplementary search string in this review: a separate Vespidae–entomopathogen literature search was already completed.

## Research question

What evidence exists for the efficacy, strain traits, formulation/delivery, horizontal transmission, non-target risk, and regulatory readiness of _Beauveria bassiana_ when applied via selective traps, bait stations, or autodissemination devices that deliver spores in fluid or protein carriers, such that primarily the target insect acquires and vectors the pathogen?

## Subquestions

**01-existing-products** What commercial or named experimental _B. bassiana_ products, formulations, or trap/bait/autodissemination systems are registered, marketed, or reported with product codes, and which use liquid, oil, protein, granular, biopolymer, encapsulation, seed-coating, or bait-based delivery? Search as two logged strings: market catalogue (all registered/commercial products and strains) and delivery systems (trap/bait/autodissemination devices).

**02-efficacy-mechanics-delivery**

- **02a_efficacy** What mortality, time-to-death, or population effects do _B. bassiana_ strains produce when delivered via traps, baits, or autodissemination devices? Report speed relative to quarantine or statutory timelines when studies provide it; do not require that speed for inclusion.
- **02b_strains_traits** Which strain traits (Pr1/Pr2 proteases, chitinases, hydrophobins, thermotolerance, UV tolerance, adhesion) enable effective cuticle breach and survival after transfer from fluid/protein carriers or trap surfaces?
- **02c_formulation_delivery** How do fluid, proteinaceous, oil, wettable powder, granular, microgranular, hydrogel, or microencapsulated carriers affect spore viability, adhesion, germination, persistence, and transfer efficiency from traps, bait stations, autodissemination devices, baits, or contaminated surfaces under field conditions?

**03-autodissemination-social** How do social behaviours (allogrooming, necrophoresis, trophallaxis) and nest hygiene affect horizontal spore transfer from foragers that contacted a treated trap or bait to nestmates and the queen?

**04-nontarget-ecotox**

- Which physical, chemical, or seasonal attractants and trap/bait/disseminator designs maximise contact by the target species while minimising exposure of non-target pollinators (_Apis_, _Bombus_, native bees) and beneficial arthropods?
- What is the environmental persistence, secondary transmission risk, toxicity, and sublethal behavioral effects of _B. bassiana_ strains on non-target arthropods when spores are delivered via trap or bait matrices, autodissemination or disseminator devices, bee-vectoring, or sterile-insect dissemination pathways?

**05-regulatory-policy** What EU active-substance approval pathways, data requirements, and emergency or quarantine authorisation options exist for _B. bassiana_ under Regulation (EC) No 1107/2009 (Netherlands rapporteur dossiers where reported), and do existing representative uses cover trap, bait-station, or autodissemination delivery?

**06-background-proxies** (No primary sub-question — reserve for general biology, pathogenesis, proxy species, and methods papers that are **not** directly about _B. bassiana_ + traps/baits/autodissemination.)
