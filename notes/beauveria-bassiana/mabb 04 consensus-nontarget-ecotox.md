# **Targeted delivery** of _B. bassiana_ reduces but does not eliminate non-target risks to pollinators and beneficial arthropods

Targeted delivery methods—autodissemination via sterile insects, bee-vectoring, and disseminator devices—generally expose fewer non-target organisms than broadcast sprays, but sublethal effects and strain-dependent pathogenicity remain documented concerns [Flores2013](https://consensus.app/papers/sterile-males-of-ceratitis-capitata-diptera-tephritidae-flores-campos/688d4e5368c85a4a9153594b3a62a329/)[Coates2023](https://consensus.app/papers/apivectoring-of-entomopathogen-beauveria-bassiana-coates-labb%C3%A9/31a0152000e55f9db5464111e5aca9ac/)[Shipp2012](https://consensus.app/papers/effect-of-beevectored-beauveria-bassiana-on-greenhouse-shipp-kapongo/cf7e54d780bd53bfbd8e2808723003eb/).

## Pollinator Safety Under Targeted Delivery

Sterile medfly males inoculated with _B. bassiana_ and released over 7,000 ha transmitted spores to 44% of wild conspecifics through mating interactions, yet no spore transfer was detected in honey bees, coffee berry borer, or Varroa mites sampled in the release area [Flores2013](https://consensus.app/papers/sterile-males-of-ceratitis-capitata-diptera-tephritidae-flores-campos/688d4e5368c85a4a9153594b3a62a329/). Similarly, _B. bassiana_ disseminator devices deployed alongside sterile insect releases in Mexico were explicitly designed to distribute conidia with **minimal impact on non-target species**, and no non-target infections were reported [Montoya2020](https://consensus.app/papers/simultaneous-use-of-sit-plus-disseminator-devices-of-montoya-flores/0016105f9dc6596cb3d045f6e7898eb9/).

Bumblebee-vectored _B. bassiana_ in greenhouse strawberry production resulted in under 16% mortality attributable to fungal infection in carrier _Bombus impatiens_ [Coates2023](https://consensus.app/papers/apivectoring-of-entomopathogen-beauveria-bassiana-coates-labb%C3%A9/31a0152000e55f9db5464111e5aca9ac/). In greenhouse tomato and sweet pepper, bee-vectored _B. bassiana_ did not significantly increase bumblebee mortality relative to controls [Shipp2012](https://consensus.app/papers/effect-of-beevectored-beauveria-bassiana-on-greenhouse-shipp-kapongo/cf7e54d780bd53bfbd8e2808723003eb/), and bumblebee entomovectoring in oat fields had no detected effect on ladybug natural enemies [Kapongo2025](https://consensus.app/papers/sustainable-control-of-the-bird-cherryoat-aphid-kapongo-kotomale/0ab7acdb3f1f5707a012a0c71a58bd6a/).

However, sublethal effects in honey bees are documented even when mortality is negligible. Exposed honey bees displayed **enhanced sucrose responsiveness** and were twice as inconsistent in sucrose perception, responding to lower but not higher concentrations [Carlesso2020](https://consensus.app/papers/exposure-to-a-biopesticide-interferes-with-sucrose-carlesso-smargiassi/12e08e8859f45e09a5410f6f176f56e5/). Because sucrose responsiveness governs division of foraging labor, these changes could unsettle the numerical ratio between forager sub-castes and lead to suboptimal colony foraging [Carlesso2020](https://consensus.app/papers/exposure-to-a-biopesticide-interferes-with-sucrose-carlesso-smargiassi/12e08e8859f45e09a5410f6f176f56e5/). _B. bassiana_ also altered the **cuticular hydrocarbon profile** of honey bees, disrupting nestmate recognition [Carlesso2020](https://consensus.app/papers/exposure-to-a-biopesticide-interferes-with-sucrose-carlesso-smargiassi/12e08e8859f45e09a5410f6f176f56e5/)[Morda2024](https://consensus.app/papers/safety-of-the-entomopathogenic-fungus-beauveria-bassiana-morda-nuvoli/1404ddaa0ec45a358e94887ae03ef374/).

## Beneficial Arthropod Compatibility

Bee-vectored _B. bassiana_ showed no significant mortality differences from controls for the parasitoids _Encarsia formosa_, _Eretmocerus eremicus_, _Aphidius colemani_, or the predatory mite _Amblyseius swirskii_, and parasitism and predation levels were unaffected [Shipp2012](https://consensus.app/papers/effect-of-beevectored-beauveria-bassiana-on-greenhouse-shipp-kapongo/cf7e54d780bd53bfbd8e2808723003eb/). The lacewing _Chrysoperla lucasina_ showed only slight survival and development differences when exposed to _B. bassiana_ at field concentrations, with no significant impact on adult reproductive performance [Morda2024](https://consensus.app/papers/safety-of-the-entomopathogenic-fungus-beauveria-bassiana-morda-nuvoli/1404ddaa0ec45a358e94887ae03ef374/). The egg parasitoid _Telenomus podisi_ maintained parasitism capacity after exposure to _B. bassiana_ isolates, though sex ratio and emergence were influenced [Da Silva2026](https://consensus.app/papers/potential-of-beauveria-bassiana-in-the-control-of-silva-carneiro/0d1c3841380d5c19ba2a745e934c00f6/).

Notably, the predatory bug _O. insidiosus_ experienced significantly greater mortality under bee-vectored _B. bassiana_ compared to controls, indicating that even targeted delivery can harm certain beneficial species [Shipp2012](https://consensus.app/papers/effect-of-beevectored-beauveria-bassiana-on-greenhouse-shipp-kapongo/cf7e54d780bd53bfbd8e2808723003eb/). The predatory paper wasp _Polistes dominula_ suffered increased mortality, altered locomotion and feeding, selective larval ejection, reduced oviposition, and colony failure after topical exposure to field-realistic concentrations of the commercial strain ATCC 74040 [Cappa2023](https://consensus.app/papers/adverse-effects-of-the-fungal-biopesticide-beauveria-cappa-fazi/602616b4d3ca5bb8a1389edbb4124ed7/). In broadcast spray contexts, internal _B. bassiana_ CFU in spiders and carabid beetles were low and none of 2,500 incubated arthropods were colonized, suggesting minor and short-lived impact [Goettel2021](https://consensus.app/papers/measurement-of-internal-beauveria-bassiana-to-ascertain-goettel-inglis/9759d9f845845d1ebf81df6ae31d9c50/).

## Strain and Context Dependence

Safety outcomes vary substantially by strain, formulation, and environmental conditions. _B. bassiana_ strain B4 reduced populations of the predatory mite _Amblyseius cucumeris_ and killed adults of the parasitoid _Anastatus japonicus_, prompting the recommendation to avoid releasing these natural enemies simultaneously with fungal application [Li2024](https://consensus.app/papers/toxicity-of-beauveria-bassiana-to-bactrocera-dorsalis-and-li-zhang/5c92b958df775c49ad3540dba96f9d65/). The PfBb strain infected all six larval instars of the non-target _Spodoptera frugiperda_, with virulence decreasing as larval instar increased [Gao2022](https://consensus.app/papers/pathogenicity-of-beauveria-bassiana-pfbb-and-immune-gao-luo/68c1a6f760d85f1ab4d0b05872b6a589/). An oil-based formulation caused 76.6% mortality in _Apis mellifera caucasica_ adults at the highest dose (1 × 10⁹ spores/mL) but minimal effects at lower doses [Biryol2025](https://consensus.app/papers/target-and-nontarget-impact-of-oilbased-beauveria-biryol-say%C4%B1n/30f8654e6d0751b9bfc85e4a2aec603a/).

Hive internal temperatures of approximately 35°C can inhibit _B. bassiana_ conidial germination, limiting colony-level spread even when foragers transport spores back to the hive [Biryol2025](https://consensus.app/papers/target-and-nontarget-impact-of-oilbased-beauveria-biryol-say%C4%B1n/30f8654e6d0751b9bfc85e4a2aec603a/). This thermal barrier partially explains why field studies generally find no epizootics in managed hives [Carlesso2020](https://consensus.app/papers/exposure-to-a-biopesticide-interferes-with-sucrose-carlesso-smargiassi/12e08e8859f45e09a5410f6f176f56e5/). The broad host range of _B. bassiana_ across at least 15 insect orders means that strain selectivity and environmental competence—particularly temperature and humidity requirements for germination—are critical determinants of non-target safety under any delivery method [Keller2023](https://consensus.app/papers/efficacy-and-nontarget-effects-of-broadcast-treatments-to-keller-walsh/f2268ce7dc925dbab3de56581d8c6ead/).

Targeted delivery approaches thus reduce non-target exposure by limiting spore distribution to pest-occupied zones, but they do not universally eliminate risk: sublethal behavioral effects in honey bees, mortality in specific predators, and strain-dependent pathogenicity to non-target species persist as documented concerns.
